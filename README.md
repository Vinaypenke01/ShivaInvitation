# Shiva Kumar & Lokeshwari Devi — Wedding Invitation

An interactive digital Indian wedding invitation celebrating the union of **Chi. Shiva Kumar** and **Chi.La.Sow. Lokeshwari Devi** on **Thursday, 26 November 2026 at 9:16 a.m. ("Dhanur Lagnam")** at **Parimala Convention Hall, Narayanaguda, Hyderabad**.

## Features

- **Interactive Opening Experience**: Traditional entrance animation with auspicious mantras, Lord Ganesha, and mango-leaf toranam.
- **Bilingual Support (English & తెలుగు)**: Instant toggle between English and traditional Telugu *Subhalekha* wording.
- **Sumuhurtham & Venue Details**: Full details for the Dhanur Lagnam ceremony, parents/hosts (*Sri Guthula Raviteja & Smt. Vijaya Devi*, *Sri Medishetty Govindu & Smt. Bhavani*), and one-tap Google Calendar integration.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining until the Sumuhurtham.
- **Direct Google Maps Navigation**: Direct route pin to Parimala Convention Hall, Barkatpura Road, Chitrapuri Colony, Narayanaguda, Hyderabad.
